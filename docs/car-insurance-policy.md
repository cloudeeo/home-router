# Car insurance policy

Alpine Shield Insurance (fictional). Policy number AS-2026-04417. Insured vehicle: VW Golf, plate GE 000 000 (sample). Policy period: 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026.

## 1. Cover

The policy includes third-party liability, partial cover (fire, theft, natural hazards, glass breakage, collision with animals) and full collision cover with a bonus-protection option.

## 4. Windscreen and glass damage

Damage to the windscreen, side windows and rear window is covered under partial cover. A repair of a chip or small crack is free of excess. A full replacement of the windscreen has an excess of CHF 200. Glass damage does not affect the no-claims bonus. Headlights and mirrors are not considered glass.

## 6. Exclusions

Damage caused while driving under the influence, during races or by an unlicensed driver is not covered. Wear and tear is not covered.

## 8. How to make a claim

Report the damage within 30 days through the Alpine Shield app or by phone at 0800 000 000 (sample). For glass damage, a partner garage can bill the insurer directly.
