# My notes: garden fence

Notes written after the walk round the garden in early October.

The east fence leans badly between the third and fifth posts; two boards are rotten at the bottom. The neighbour on the left said he would share the cost of the part along his side.

Ideas: use larch boards, which last without treatment. Ask the carpenter recommended by the garden committee. Measure the length before asking for quotes: roughly 9 metres.

Reminder to self: check whether the rental contract says who pays for fence repairs.
