# Rental contract for the flat

Lease between Rive Gauche Properties (landlord, fictional) and the tenant for a 4-room flat, Rue des Tilleuls 12, third floor (sample address).

## Term

The lease starts on 1 April 2024 and runs for a fixed term until 31 March 2027. After that date it renews automatically for one year at a time unless one party gives notice.

## Notice

Either party may give notice with three months' notice, for the end of March, June or September. Notice must be given in writing by registered letter.

## Rent and charges

Monthly rent is CHF 2,150, plus CHF 180 for heating and service charges, payable in advance by the first day of each month. The deposit is CHF 6,450, held in a blocked bank account.

## Maintenance

Small repairs up to CHF 150 are the tenant's responsibility. Pets are allowed with the landlord's written consent.
