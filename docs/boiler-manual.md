# ThermoMax 24 boiler: quick manual

ThermoMax 24 combi gas boiler (fictional model). Quick reference for residents.

## Page 14: resetting the boiler

If the display shows a flashing flame symbol or error E01, press and hold the reset button for 5 seconds. Wait two minutes. If the error comes back three times, do not keep resetting: call the service number.

## Page 16: error codes

E01: no flame detected. E02: water pressure too low, top up to 1.5 bar. E05: temperature sensor fault, call service. E09: flue blocked, switch off and call service.

## Page 20: yearly service

The boiler must be serviced once a year by a certified technician. The last service was on 3 March 2026.
