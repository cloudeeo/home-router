# Gym membership contract

Lake Fit Club (fictional). Annual membership, renewed every 1 January.

## Fees

The annual fee is CHF 790, payable in one instalment or in twelve monthly instalments of CHF 69.

## Notice period

The membership renews automatically for one year. To cancel, send written notice at least two months before the end of the membership year, that is by 31 October. Cancellations received later take effect at the end of the following year.

## Pausing

The membership can be paused for up to three months a year for illness, pregnancy or travel abroad, with supporting documents.
